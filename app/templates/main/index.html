Questions, answered

Know what happens before you join.

Why do I need both an email and mobile number? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

Your email secures your website account. Your mobile number is matched with the number you choose to share through Telegram, so only a registered student can activate job alerts.

Will Job Matcher apply for me? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

No. You review every recommendation and choose whether to continue to the employer’s application page.

Can I pause Telegram alerts? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

Yes. Alert timing, frequency, pausing, renewal, and disconnection remain available in Settings.

Does a high match score guarantee an interview? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

No. It explains alignment with the information available in your profile and the listing. Hiring decisions always belong to the employer.
